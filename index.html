01 — About

A short introduction

I am a postgraduate student studying Robotics at the University of Manchester, with a background that spans robotics autonomy, machine learning and software engineering. Most of my work starts from a simple question — can the system work out what to do by itself? — whether that is a robot mapping a room it has never seen, or a game AI that out-manoeuvres a human player.

Alongside robotics I build the software around the research: 3D interfaces in Three.js, desktop and Android applications, and responsive web front-ends. I enjoy owning the whole path from algorithm to something polished enough that other people can pick it up and play with it.

Career objective. To work as a robotics and AI engineer building autonomous systems that combine perception, planning and learning — progressing towards leading projects that bring robotics and interactive, human-facing software together.

Projects & research work

Game AI · Three.js

Implementation of Blocks World with AI — Dueler

Individual project Video · Report

A 3D take on the classic Blocks World planning puzzle, built with Three.js, in which a trained AI agent competes against a human player rather than simply solving the puzzle on its own. The agent reasons about both the block stacks and its opponent's goal: it will take blocks the human player needs, and play deliberate feints and decoys to break their plan. The project supports single-player, local multiplayer and human-vs-AI modes on an animated board that makes every move easy to read.

Game Dev · Unreal

First-Person Shooter built in Unreal Engine

Individual project Video demo

A first-person shooter built in Unreal Engine with several playable maps across both single-player and multiplayer modes. The emphasis was on combat feel: reliable hit detection when firing, reactive feedback for landing hits, and clear damage feedback for the player when they are hit — supported by health and HUD elements that keep the state of a firefight readable at a glance.

Robotics · Bug1

Arena Exploration & Mapping with the Bug1 Algorithm

Research project Video · Report

Research into autonomous exploration and occupancy-grid map construction of unknown arenas, using a Pioneer 3-DX robot in the Webots simulation environment. The core task was to design a navigation strategy and collision-avoidance mechanism that let the robot explore without any preloaded map information. I iterated the anti-collision monitoring logic and combined the Bug1 algorithm with PID control to achieve stable navigation, which produced high-accuracy occupancy grid maps for both the simple and complex test arenas.
